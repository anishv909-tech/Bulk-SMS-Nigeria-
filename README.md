## Promotional, Transactional, and OTP Messaging

Bulk SMS platforms can support different categories of business communication.

### Promotional SMS
Businesses use promotional messages to share special offers, discounts, product launches, and marketing announcements with customers who have opted in.

### Transactional SMS
Transactional messages provide useful information about customer activities, including order updates, payment confirmations, and service notifications.

### OTP SMS
One-time passwords help verify users during registration, login, and other authentication processes.

Using the appropriate message category helps businesses keep their communication relevant and organized.
